Horizon Tasks — Privacy policy

Horizon Tasks is a personal task planner for a single user, its owner.

  • Google sign-in: used only to confirm who you are. The app receives your name and email address and nothing else. It does not access your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or contacts.
  • Your data: the tasks you enter are stored in a private Supabase database, readable only by your own signed-in account.
  • On your device: a copy of your tasks (and any changes made offline) is kept in this browser so the app works without a connection. Signing out erases it.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Deletion: contact the owner through the GitHub repository to have an account and its data deleted.
